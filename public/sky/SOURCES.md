The texture derives from the [ESO publication JPEG](https://cdn.eso.org/images/publicationjpg/eso0932a.jpg) (4000 × 2000, 4,857,484 bytes, SHA-256 `5363732a1629eed9df2f707b31eaae6b117c0ee35d7cc8d6ddd636bc6512302d`). It was resized to 3072 × 1536 with Lanczos, adjusted with the 8-bit channel curve `max(0, round((channel − 7) × 255 / 248))`, and encoded as WebP at quality 85, method 6.

The 946,914-byte WebP has SHA-256 `5709fcf473a5587b979e3057f563738daa41a6bf81f73e0370718ad109ed85b7`. The simulator applies a cool color grade and artistic orientation at render time. It draws interactive bodies and the additional star field separately.
